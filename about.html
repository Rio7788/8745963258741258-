Good fruit. Good people. Good days.

We started as a tiny cart with one blender and a bag of oranges. People kept coming back, so we opened a small shop and kept the same rule: use real fruit and make everything fresh.

Today we squeeze juices, blend smoothies, toast sandwiches and bake cookies and cake. The menu changes a little with the fruit season, so ask what is sweetest this week.
